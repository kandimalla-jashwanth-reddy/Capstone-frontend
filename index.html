See an issue in your city? Report it in seconds.

CrowdCivics lets residents capture potholes, broken streetlights, overflowing bins and more using a simple, mobile-first interface. Your reports go straight to the right municipal department for faster action.

  • 📍 Automatic location tagging for every report
  • 📷 Attach photos for better context
  • 📊 Track the status of your reports from submission to resolution
  • 🗺️ City dashboard helps staff prioritize hotspots and bottlenecks

Administrative Dashboard
