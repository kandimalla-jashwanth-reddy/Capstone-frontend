Tips for a Good Report

  • Take clear, well-lit photos of the issue
  • Enable GPS for accurate location detection
  • Describe severity and any safety risks clearly
  • Include nearby landmarks in your description
  • Check if the issue is already reported nearby

What Happens Next?

  • Your report is reviewed within 24 hours
  • Assigned to the appropriate department
  • You receive status update notifications
  • Average resolution time: 3–7 working days
